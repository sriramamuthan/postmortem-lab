### 01 · Cold open

June fourth, nineteen ninety-six. Koo-roo, French Guiana. <break time="0.6s" />
Europe's brand-new rocket, Ariane Five, lifts off on its very first flight. <break time="1.0s" />
Thirty-seven seconds later, it breaks apart. <break time="0.5s" />
Hundreds of millions of dollars. Gone. <break time="1.0s" />
Not because of an engine. Not because of a weld. <break time="0.4s" />
Because of one number, that didn't fit in its box. <break time="1.2s" />

### 02 · The limit

This box holds sixteen bits. <break time="0.3s" />
The biggest number it can store is thirty-two thousand, seven hundred and sixty-seven. <break time="1.0s" />
Go one past that, <break time="0.5s" /> and you've just recreated one of the most expensive software bugs in history. <break time="1.5s" />

### 03 · What this is

I'm Ram, and this is Post-Mortem Lab. <break time="0.8s" />
Here's the idea. Every episode, I take one famous engineering failure, and I rebuild it. <break time="0.4s" />
Not a diagram. Not a slideshow. <break time="0.3s" />
The actual mechanism, as code you can run in your browser. <break time="0.8s" />
You hear the story. You trigger the bug yourself. <break time="0.4s" />
Then we fix it, and prove the fix with a test. <break time="0.4s" />
And you walk away with one lesson you'll recognise the next time it shows up in your own work. <break time="1.2s" />
